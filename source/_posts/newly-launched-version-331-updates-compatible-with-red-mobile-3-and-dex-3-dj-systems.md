## Newly Launched Version 3.3.1 Updates: Compatible with RED Mobile 3 and DEX 3 DJ Systems

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/version331-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/version331-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "version331-coverimage")

#### 3.3.1 Updates for DEX 3 and RED Mobile 3 now available for download

Fresh out of the bat-cave, new [DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) maintenance updates (_with a couple new changes_) are now available! Since [RED Mobile 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) are essentially all-new, built from the ground up, you can expect updates often — once every 30-45 days, like clockwork. As has been the case since RED Mobile 2 and DEX 2, all updates are free of charge.

[![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/thevideopooldex3promo-coverimage.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "thevideopooldex3promo-coverimage")](https://pcdj.com/dex3-the-video-pool-offer/)

The most notable inclusion in 3.3.1 is support for three new Reloop DJ controllers: The Reloop Beatmix 2, the Reloop Beatmix 4 and the Reloop Terminal Mix 8\. We’ve found these [DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) as excellent options for Red Mobile 3 and DEX 3 users.

**Here’s what’s new, changed or fixed in our DJ software updates:**

_Added:_

* Reloop Beatmix 2 Now Supported
* Reloop Beatmix 4 Now Supported
* Reloop Terminal Mix 8 Now Supported

_Changes/Updates:_

* Waveforms amplitude normalized
* Allows using special characters in lists names (eg. &)
* Fixed several bugs
* Denon DN-MC6000 improvements
* Minor improvements
