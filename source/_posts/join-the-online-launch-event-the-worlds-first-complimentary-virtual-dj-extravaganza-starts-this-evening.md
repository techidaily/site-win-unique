## Join the Online Launch Event: The World's First Complimentary Virtual DJ Extravaganza Starts This Evening!

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/djexpo-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/djexpo-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "djexpo-coverimage")

The **Virtual DJ Expo** (<http://www.djnvirtualexpo.com>) coordinated by [Disc Jockey News](http://discjockeynews.com/) kicks-off tonight, and you can attend online completely **FREE** of charge.  
  
  
PCDJ is a proud sponsor of July’s Virtual DJ Expo, and 3 lucky attendees will walk away with copies of our RED Mobile 3 and DEX 3 [DJ mixing software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) programs!

If you’re a seasoned **Mobile DJ** or just starting out, you do not want to miss this opportunity to learn from some of the **best in the business**. The **[Virtual DJ Expo](http://www.djnvirtualexpo.com)** is THREE nights of great DJ related content, all for FREE and right from your own computer or mobile device. This is a DJ convention, _from your couch._ 

Tonight’s pre-show starts at 8:00 pm central, the show starts at 9:00 pm central.
