## Selecting Ideal UpLight Fixtures - DJ Tips with PCDJ

[![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/choosinguplighting-blog.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/choosinguplighting-blog.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "choosinguplighting-blog")

Some weeks back we posted about [getting started with uplighting](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), which when done properly can dynamically change any event space into a visually stunning environment that will wow your client. Mobile DJs everywhere are adding uplighting packages to their services.

We just stumbled across the _**“Choosing Your Uplighting”**_ video below created by DJ [JLDJUK](https://www.youtube.com/user/JLDJUK "JLDJUK") (_Joseph_) and feel it’s a great follow up, focusing on what types of uplighting you should have in your arsenal.

_The main point:_ If you’re just getting started you don’t want to invest your money in just one type of uplighting, but rather purchase various types for different applications. JLDJUK explains the different types of uplighting and what they are best suited for.
