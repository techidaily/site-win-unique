## Latest JammText Update Released: Version 1.3 with Enhanced Capabilities

[![JammText Version 1.3 email to screen](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/jammtext-v13.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/jammtext-v13.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "JammText Version 1.3 email to screen")

[JammText](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) is the premier interactive text-to-screen software for DJs — version 1.3 introduces a few new highly-requested features.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![JammText Screens](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/JammText-3-Screen-image.jpg?fit=300%2C181&ssl=1 "JammText Screens")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/JammText-3-Screen-image.jpg?fit=845%2C509&ssl=1)

Mixcity Inc, announced yesterday a new version 1.3 release of their JammText software for DJs and karaoke hosts. Included in the free update for JammText customers is 64bit support that improves system-wide performance and support for the latest macOS Mojave. A widely requested addition is the ability for patrons to email pictures or messages to screen (as opposed to only text-to-screen support).

MixCity Inc also announced that another highly sought after feature is well under way and should be released soon. Read the message below from Mixcity Inc for further details.

JammText allows DJs to interact with their audience while boosting your marketing and revenue potential. You can learn more about JammText and download a free trial [HERE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/).

### Email to screen

We are excited to announce that we have added a new email to screen function in JammText 1.3\. Your audience will now have the option of emailing a picture or message to the screen.

### Scan to screen

With the new email to screen feature we have also added an auto generated QR code. This QR code is randomly displayed on your screen instructing the audience to scan code to post pictures or messages to this screen. When the QR code is scanned it automatically opens the audience members email app with a composed email addressed to post on the JammText screen.

### Instagram discontinued

Facebook has decided to make changes to their Instagram platform. These changes went into effect today, December 11, 2018\. Instagram will no longer allow third-party apps to search for pictures via a hashtag. JammText is one of the third-party apps that has been negatively effected by this decision. Mixcity Inc. has no choice but to discontinue the use of Instagram in JammText. This decision only affects the use of Instagram in JammText. Every other part of JammText will continue to work as it always has. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause to you or any of our customer base. We encourage you to continue using JammText text-to-screen, Tweet-to-screen and the addition of email-to-screen to engage and interact with your audience!
